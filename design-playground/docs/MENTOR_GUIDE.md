# Mentor Guide — Facilitating DESIGN TO UI/UX

Design Playground doubles seamlessly as a **complete teaching deck and classroom facilitation console**—eliminating external slide decks.

---

## 1. Using Presentation Mode

Click the **Present** button in the top navigation bar (or **Present Module** / **Present Figma Guide** inside modules and the Figma Guide page):

- **Single-Viewport Edge-to-Edge CRAFT Presentation Deck (38 Slides · `12:30 – 17:00`):** Presentation Mode fills the screen edge-to-edge (`100%` stage width & height, `100dvh`, zero vertical scrolling at `1280×800` & `1440×900`) with high-contrast **CRAFT — DESIGN TO UI/UX** presentation canvases (*"From Grid, Typography, and Color to Functional Interface Design"*) rendered in Soft Off-White (`#F2F2F2`) or Charcoal (`#171717`) across **8 Sections (`Slides 01–38`)**, featuring an expanded **7-slide beginner Figma Guide (`Slides 22–28`)** alongside 4 learning stages (`01 Understand -> 02 Organize -> 03 Translate -> 04 Create & Review`).
- **Keyboard, Clicker & Fullscreen Navigation:**
  - `F` (or **`Fullscreen (F)`** button): Toggle true browser Fullscreen (`requestFullscreen()` / `exitFullscreen()` with seamless viewport fallback) while automatically hiding top/bottom bars into a compact floating bottom-right control dock (`Prev`, `Next`, `Notes`, `Exit Fullscreen`, `X`)
  - `ArrowRight` / `PageDown` / `Space`: Advance to the next slide (`Space` ignores interactive buttons/inputs)
  - `ArrowLeft` / `PageUp`: Return to the previous slide
  - `N`: Open/Close the floating **Mentor Notes / Presenter Support Drawer** featuring concise Indonesian presenter facilitation prompts (`1. Yang Perlu Dijelaskan`, `2. Poin Utama Peserta`, `3. Contoh & Live Demo Praktis`, `4. Pertanyaan untuk Peserta`) while visible slides remain clean English
  - `H`: Hide / Restore top and bottom navigation chrome manually without leaving Fullscreen or changing normal mode
  - `Escape`: Close Mentor Notes Drawer if open -> Exit Fullscreen -> or close Presentation Mode cleanly back to normal browsing
- **Embedded Live Demonstrations (`CraftSlideDemos` + `MiniFigmaExercise`):** Key concept slides are paired directly with live interactive demos (`Live Class Vote`, `Decoration vs Design`, `Element Spotlight`, `Grid Overlay`, `Layout Switchboard`, `Typography Switchboard`, `Interactive Color Wheel`, `Contrast Switchboard` with WCAG math, `7-Point Hero Anatomy` for `FORM / STUDIO`, `UI/UX Decision Switchboard`, **7-part Figma Guide interactive walkthroughs** for Intro/Fundamentals Bridge, 4-Area Workspace Explorer, 6 Essential Tools `V/F/R/O/T/Shift+A`, Live Properties Inspector + Layers, Without vs With Auto Layout Comparison, 6-Step `FORM / STUDIO` Hero Progression + Live Hero Sandbox, Quick Reference Cheat Sheet, and sprint checklist).

Inside any `/learn/:chapterId` module page or `/challenges` brief page, mentors can click **Mentor Notes** / **Show Mentor Review Guide** to reveal structured facilitation blocks (`Timing`, `What to Teach`, `What to Say`, `What to Show`, `What Students Do`, `Questions to Ask`, `Expected Outcomes`, and Challenge `Common Beginner Pitfalls`). ## 3. Session Pacing & Key Classroom Prompts

1. **09:00 Opening & 09:20 M01:** Open with *"Have you ever seen a design that looks beautiful yet fails to communicate its message?"* Walk students through Poster A vs. Poster B live before explaining visual hierarchy.
2. **10:15 M03 Rapid Drill (15 min):** Focus firmly on the **5 Core Principles** (`Contrast`, `Balance`, `Visual Hierarchy`, `Alignment`, `Proximity`) right before break so students leave with an instant bug-hunting lens.
3. **10:45 M04 Reality Check:** Show ColorLab live testing `#F5F3EE` on `#C6FF3D` (`1.07:1` Fail) vs. `#171717` on `#C6FF3D` (`15.17:1` AAA), and reinforce that color meanings depend on culture and context rather than rigid stereotypes.
4. **11:20 M05 Swiss Editorial Clarification:** Show the colorful Specimens in the **Swiss Editorial Gallery** and toggle grid overlays ON/OFF so students internalize that Swiss Editorial = clarity + grids + boldness (never monotone black-and-white boredom).
5. **12:30 M06 & 13:05 M07 Figma Transition:** Keep tools lean (`V, F, R, O, T, P, Shift+A, Ctrl/Cmd+G`), emphasize Frame (`F`) nesting in Layers, and dedicate the final 10–12 minutes of M07 to completing the 5-part **Mini Figma Exercise**.
6. **13:40 & 14:40 Challenges:** Direct students to **Mid-Fidelity Blueprints** (spatial structure only) and **Social Media Assets / Portfolio Assets** (`/assets`), reminding everyone not to copy finished pixels verbatim.
7. **15:40 Critique & Reflection:** Grade peer/student walkthroughs verbally against weighted rubrics (`25/20/20/20/15%` and `25/20/20/15/20%`), asking each student to defend **why** their hierarchy and color pairings serve the audience.
