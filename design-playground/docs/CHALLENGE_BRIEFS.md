# Practical Workshop Challenge Briefs — Design Playground

Phase 03 (**Make It Real**) translates morning graphic foundations (`Modules 01–05`) and midday Figma skills (`Modules 06–07`) into two self-contained, portfolio-ready Figma challenges with complete client profiles, required text copy, middle-fidelity layout blueprints, curated downloadable starter assets, weighted rubrics, and gated Mentor Critique Guides.

## Challenge 01 — Graphic Design: CREATIVE FREQUENCY Poster (`challenge-01`)

- **Schedule Slot:** `13:40 – 14:25` (45 minutes hands-on sprint)
- **Route:** `/challenges/creative-frequency-poster`
- **Tool & Canvas Spec:** Figma A3 Portrait (`297 × 420 mm` · `842 × 1191 px` in Figma)
- **Design Direction:** Swiss Editorial Poster Design — structured grid (`4` or `6` columns, `48px` outer margin, `20px` gutter), strong typographic scale contrast, purposeful vibrant accent color (not restricted to black & white), and disciplined negative space.
- **Asset Pack:** Social Media Assets (`/assets` tab `poster`)

### Approved Poster Copy
| Field | Required Text |
|---|---|
| **Event Title** | `CREATIVE FREQUENCY` |
| **Tagline** | `A Celebration of Ideas, Art, and Music.` |
| **Date & Time** | `20 December 2026` · `10:00 AM – 08:00 PM` |
| **Venue / Location** | `Creative Space, Bandung` |
| **Supporting Copy** | `Join creators, designers, musicians, and visual storytellers for a full-day celebration of creativity, inspiration, and collaboration.` |
| **Call to Action** | `Be Part of the Experience` |

### Weighted Review Rubric (100%)
1. **Visual Hierarchy (25%)** — `CREATIVE FREQUENCY` commands unmistakable Tier 1 dominance over logistics and CTA.
2. **Typography & Readability (20%)** — Expressive display tight leading paired with legible supporting body copy and dates.
3. **Color Contrast & Mood (20%)** — Vibrant modern energy preserving clean WCAG contrast across every text line.
4. **Layout, Grid & Alignment (20%)** — Crisp outer margins (`~48px`) and column snapping in Figma.
5. **Visual Unity & Balance (15%)** — Soundwave motif, geometry, and typography unify into one cohesive A3 piece.

### Middle-Fidelity Structural Reference Zones
- Top Metadata Strip (Location + Date badge)
- Dominant Focal Title Block (`CREATIVE FREQUENCY` + Tagline)
- Center Visual Stage (Soundwave / Acoustic Geometry centerpiece)
- Lower Two-Column Grid Split (Logistics Box + Supporting Description + CTA Badge)

## Challenge 02 — UI Design: Personal Portfolio Website Hero (`challenge-02`)

- **Schedule Slot:** `14:40 – 15:40` (60 minutes hands-on sprint)
- **Route:** `/challenges/personal-portfolio-hero`
- **Tool & Canvas Spec:** Figma Desktop (`1440 × 1024 px`) + Optional Mobile stack preview (`390 × 844 px`)
- **Design Direction:** Clean, approachable digital interface hero sitting completely above the fold on laptop/desktop screens (`12-column` grid, `64px–80px` side margins, `24px` gutter, `8px` spacing steps, Auto Layout nav & CTA buttons).
- **Asset Pack:** Portfolio Assets (`/assets` tab `portfolio`)

### Fictional Client Profile
- **Name:** `Alex Morgan`
- **Role:** `Digital Designer`
- **Specializations:** `Graphic Design · UI/UX Design · Visual Identity`
- **Brand Tagline:** `Creating meaningful visual experiences through design, experimentation, and digital storytelling.`

### Approved UI Copy & Elements
| Slot | Required Text |
|---|---|
| **Navbar Brand & Links** | `AM` Monogram + `Home · About · Projects · Contact` |
| **Role Eyebrow / Tag** | `Alex Morgan — Digital Designer` |
| **Hero Display Headline (H1)** | `DESIGNING IDEAS INTO DIGITAL EXPERIENCES.` |
| **Hero Subheadline** | `I'm Alex, a digital designer exploring the intersection of visual communication, interaction, and creative technology.` |
| **Primary CTA** | `Explore My Work` |
| **Secondary CTA** | `Get In Touch` |

### Weighted Review Rubric (100%)
1. **Above-the-Fold Clarity & Hierarchy (25%)** — Visitor immediately sees Alex Morgan's role, value proposition, and primary CTA without scrolling past `1024px`.
2. **Auto Layout & CTA Distinction (20%)** — Clean separation between Primary (`Explore My Work`) and Secondary (`Get In Touch`) actions using Auto Layout spacing (`8px` steps).
3. **Typography & Readable Measure (20%)** — Subheadline constrained comfortably (`~480px–540px`, 5–6 columns) instead of stretching across the full 1440px viewport.
4. **Navbar Alignment & Responsive Logic (15%)** — Consistent baseline alignment and believable `390×844` vertical stacking logic.
5. **Contrast & Visual Polish (20%)** — Balanced left-copy / right-portrait split with crisp `4.5:1+` contrast on interactive buttons.

## Mentor Critique Guide (Included Gated Inside `/challenges`)
- Displays common beginner mistakes to spot immediately during walkthroughs (e.g., flat hierarchy where title & venue share font weight, placing dates on noisy geometry, spanning subheadlines across all 12 columns, or giving both CTA buttons identical solid fill weight).
- Includes ready-to-ask classroom critique questions so mentors guide students to explain *why* they chose their grid, typography, contrast, and spacing.
